# Gothic Eclipse: reusable design philosophy

Version: 2026-10-02. This is an authored interpretation of the accepted Gothic Eclipse frame, not a new model analysis and not a command to make every frame gothic. Evidence: the four sessions and explicit owner acceptance in `session-lessons.md`; one direct inspection of `assets/source/gothic_eclipse_minimal_idle_2x.png`. Refresh only when the reference or owner's decisions change. Do not reattach or reanalyze that image on every generation.

## Transfer the construction, not the props

Gothic reads as an assembled physical object, not a rectangular background carrying stickers. Pillars support a lintel; footings carry weight; carved brackets turn corners; ornaments grow out of that construction. The source uses weathered dark stone, small warm fissures, an eclipse crest and candles. Those are its vocabulary, not universal requirements. In quality mode translate the construction into the requested material family (silverwork, wood, botanical carving, ceramic, etc.); do not import stone, skulls, flames or cathedral tracery unless that design actually calls for them. In explicit gothic mode retain the architectural vocabulary as described here; no image attachment is needed to recover it.

## Hierarchy and asymmetry

The broad structural silhouette reads first, medium relief second, tiny cuts and texture third. A large top crest, smaller architectural breaks, unequal corners and the lower-left candle cluster create hierarchy; long quieter spans prevent detail from becoming noise. Their exact placement is not a mandatory template. Use a few intentional concentrations of detail joined by restrained construction. Do not spread identical ornaments at regular intervals simply because a layout can tile them. Richness comes from designed form and material depth, not object count.

## Depth belongs to the material

Faces, bevels, recessed channels, occlusion and consistent highlights establish thickness. Dark recesses remain part of the material; they are not a separate broad black liner between decoration and content. Light has a local cause and reveals the relief; it does not wash the whole frame or the opening. In a pale-metal frame, differentiated edge highlights and recesses can do the same work without borrowing Gothic's orange light.

## Content is an opening, not a picture mounted on a mat

The terminal occupies the actual opening in the object. The material's inner edge meets its rectangular boundary directly. There is no second inner rectangle, moat, picture-frame mat, flat backing strip or translucent bridge. Exterior silhouette may be irregular and airy; the inner material boundary is continuous and protects the content. If an ornament needs space, recompose or thicken its supporting material, not the gap. Source aperture, material contour and reserved screen space are separate measurements.

## Adaptability is part of the drawing

Unique architecture retains its shape. Flexible length lives in deliberately calm structural runs whose cross-section, contour and lighting remain stable along their axis. A straight carved shaft can supply extra length; a sloping ribbon, star, gem or bulging scroll cannot. Transitions into a flexible run must already share its profile and tangent. Repetition must not produce a zigzag, a ladder of ornaments or periodic lighting pulses. Matching endpoint pixels alone does not establish a sound join. Preserve every meaningful object once; do not cover duplicate copies with an opaque patch.

## Motion continues an object

The accepted Gothic motion was flame around an existing wick and wax passing behind existing stone. It was not global breathing opacity. Static is the default for a new frame; animation and whole-window shaders are separate design decisions, not a way to rescue poor geometry.

## What success means

At ordinary working scale the frame should read as one crafted object touching the terminal, with clear material depth and a controlled focal hierarchy. Resizing changes calm span lengths, not sculpture proportions. A clear centre, an accepted manifest or a successful image call is not visual acceptance. Owner rejection remains authoritative.
