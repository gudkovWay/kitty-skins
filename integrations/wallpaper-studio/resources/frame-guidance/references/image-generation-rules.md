# Image-stage frame construction contract

This is the image-model-facing projection of the parent skill and quality gates. Pass this text and `gothic-philosophy.md` as actual image-generation subject content, not merely to an intermediary model. Generalize the principles; the selected design basis owns style and motifs. Generate a new artwork when requested, never reuse a rejected frame as the finished result.

Design an adaptable crafted object around one strictly rectangular, uniformly coloured opening. The solid inner material contour touches that opening on all four sides. No picture mat, second inner frame, flat backing strip, empty moat, shadow sheet or translucent bridge between ornament and opening. Thickness is actual modelled material with bevels and recesses, not an enlarged canvas or a blank border. No faces, anatomy, text, UI or terminal screenshot.

Compose a few distinctive sculptural concentrations linked by genuinely quiet structural spans. Every side must contain a substantial straight, longitudinally uniform span, around 180–256 source pixels long on the requested 1536×1024 canvas, suitable for extending that side without changing its cross-sectional profile. Preserve a steady inner AND outer contour, material thickness and lighting along these spans. They are structural craftsmanship, not featureless background liners: model the cross-sectional relief and keep its longitudinal profile stable. Broad ornament can live outside these spans. A long diagonal, waving ribbon, taper, bead, gem, sparkle, leaf or repeated distinctive carving is not a neutral span.

Quiet spans meet adjacent fixed art with matching profile, tangent, relief and light. Do not ask later slicing to manufacture missing transitions. Do not use lighting gradients along these spans which turn into periodic pulses when repeated. Detail remains rich at the chosen focal objects; the linking material stays restrained. Corners and focal art retain natural proportions. Outer silhouette can rise and vary away from the quiet spans; the protected inner boundary remains uninterrupted.

The downstream assembler measures this actual opening, preserves the complete one-shot band artwork on either side of each selected quiet span, and repeats only the neutral span directly. It does not mirror curved art, synthesize a background, move the aperture to fit ornaments, or stretch a tiny sculpture fragment to arbitrary length. Generate material that is compatible with that construction. A complete raster should also read coherently before decomposition.

The deliverable is static frame art. No global glow pulses, motion blur, motion sequences or invented window shader. Any depicted local light should have a visible object/material cause. The owner's explicit design basis overrides borrowed Gothic vocabulary, not the geometric safety requirements.

## A-tier reference: Frieren Pearl Mixed

The owner rated Gothic Eclipse S-tier and the latest Frieren Pearl Mixed A-tier after its live preview. These are artistic reference grades, not automated scores or proof that every size/animation phase is defect-free. Aim for a coherent, usable A-tier candidate; do not promise S-tier, self-award owner acceptance, or enter an unrequested endless refinement loop.

Frieren's new design is jewellery-like rather than Gothic masonry: pale silver with restrained warm rose reflections, readable bevelled rails, four pearlescent corner bodies, sculpted leaf clusters, small red gem settings, delicate bead pendants and four midpoint star ornaments. Hierarchy: corner clusters first, midpoint stars second, calm rails third. Retain material colour, luminous pearl volume and dark carved recesses; do not flatten everything into white silhouettes, global glow or a uniform palette tint. This motif set describes this reference only: another wallpaper keeps its own design language and need not acquire pearls, leaves, red gems, symmetry or exactly four stars.

The manual repair separated stationary supporting rails from one-shot sculpture. Transfer the visual principle: quiet, straight supporting material reaches the client boundary; elaborate objects remain complete and naturally proportioned, with enough outward room for their silhouettes. Midpoint ornaments must be visible over their supporting rail, not moved into the opening or into empty exterior margin. Their roots share the rail's relief and illumination. Do not depend on later masks to invent missing attachments, recover clipped tips or hide seams.

## Motion-ready art, without pretending to output an animation

When the brief explicitly requests later object animation, still deliver the single static master required by this stage. Make leaf clusters legible as coherent articulated groups with identifiable attachment roots. Keep fixed gems, settings, rails and hanging chains visually separable from moving foliage; avoid interweaving a wire through every leaf or fusing all objects into one inseparable mass. Pearl bodies need a readable complete volume and an attachment/setting; do not paint motion trails, multiple phases, detached flying leaves or pre-rendered droplets into the master.

Allow outward room around moving objects while preserving the protected rectangular opening and neutral runs. Composition must look intentional at rest, not like an exploded asset sheet. Only a later supported layer-authoring stage may produce background, petal masks, pearl bodies, sockets and foreground occluders. This image call cannot create those files by implication, and the current Studio button does not automatically author Frieren's mixed effect. Return only this stage's existing output format; no invented manifest keys or extra tool calls.
