# Global frame grammar
Protect client content; rails reach the client boundary continuously. Structural stock can tile only along its authored grain and only when explicitly permitted. Corners, fasteners, lamp, crane and other unique elements appear once at their authored anchors, never as tiled texture. Record logical/image coordinate spaces, scale, insets, outsets, pivots, sockets and minimum sizes. Cut from the approved master without painted seams, unmatched lighting, lossy masks or arbitrary stretching. Moving and fixed pieces have distinct ownership. A concept image is not an atlas or runtime implementation. Renderer capability is checked independently of application identity. Source: kitty-skin-authoring quality gates and bambooc frame-02 authored metadata.
